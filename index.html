Click the numbers to set the start
Each track is either Loop (repeats its selected clip for the whole song) or Arrange (plays only where you put it: choose a clip number below, then click or drag across bars in its lane; click a bar again to empty it). The song plays from the start marker to the end and stops. Bass, Lead, Pluck and loaded samples have a Notes button for choosing pitches. Drop an audio file on a track to replace its sound. Space = play/stop.
