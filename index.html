      Sound effects

      Drag along the top rows (bar numbers, picture, sound effects) to move the playhead; the video follows. Play runs from the playhead to the end of the song and stops.

      Each track is either Loop (repeats its selected clip for the whole song) or Arrange (plays only where you put it: choose a clip number below, then click or drag across bars in its lane; click a bar again to empty it).

      Sound effects are placed by time, not by bar. Drag one to move it (hold Shift to snap to the grid), or select it and use ← → to nudge it by one frame (1/30 s); hold Shift for 10 frames.

      Bass, Lead, Pluck and loaded samples have a Notes button for choosing pitches. Drop an audio file on a track to replace its sound. Space = play/stop.
